# EU Data Switch

The world order changed. 

[Surveillance Capitalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surveillance_capitalism)
 became a thing, turning our digital selves into product for marketeers.
Alongside the new order, data privacy got complicated because the United States and European privacy laws diverged.
The US CLOUD Act (2018) and the EU GDPR (2018) are fundamentally different.
The current administration in the United States has taken a hostile approach to foreign policy and Europe.

> Considering all this, I wanted to take a stand and remove my own personal data from organisations under US control.
